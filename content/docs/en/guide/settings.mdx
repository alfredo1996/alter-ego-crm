The Settings page lets you personalize Alter Ego CRM. Changes take effect immediately.

## Profile

Edit your display name and email address. These are shown in the sidebar and used for your account identity.

- Tap the **Edit** button next to your profile details.
- Update your name or email.
- Tap **Save Changes** to confirm.

## Theme

Choose how the app looks:

- **Light** — white background with dark text.
- **Dark** — dark background with light text.
- **System** — follows your device's appearance setting automatically.

## Plugins

Lists the plugins this deployment runs (Personal Finance, Consulting, Accounting, Rent) and which are off. The list is set where the app is deployed, with `NEXT_PUBLIC_PLUGINS`; a change applies on the next deploy.

## When Personal Finance is on

Four things join the page, and none shows with the plugin off:

- **Preferences** gain **Currency** (used for totals and new accounts; each account keeps its own) and **Language and Format** (how numbers and dates are written).
- **Categories & Tags**: **Open Categories** goes to the [Categories](/en/docs/guide/categories) page. **Tags** are labels that cut across categories. **Auto-categorization Rules** pair a **Pattern** with the **Category** to suggest (and an optional **Display as** name); each rule has a switch, and **Re-normalize All Transactions** applies them to what you already have.
- **Data** gains **Export All Data (JSON)**, one file with accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, tags and rules.
- **Danger Zone**, last on the page; each action asks you to confirm and none can be undone: **Delete All Transactions**, **Delete All Accounts & Transactions** and **Reset Categories to Default**.

## Data

- **API Keys** — generate a key for your own tools; the key is shown once.
- **Connectors** — the AI assistants connected through OAuth, and a button to disconnect them all.
