The Settings page lets you personalize Alter Ego CRM. Changes take effect immediately.

## Profile

Edit your display name and email address. These are shown in the sidebar and used for your account identity.

- Tap the **Edit** button next to your profile details.
- Update your name or email.
- Tap **Save Changes** to confirm.

## Theme

Choose how the app looks:

- **Light** — white background with dark text.
- **Dark** — dark background with light text.
- **System** — follows your device's appearance setting automatically.

## Modules

Lists the modules this deployment runs (CRM, Invoicing, Books, Solutions, Rent) and which are left out. The list is set where the app is deployed, with `NEXT_PUBLIC_DISABLED_MODULES`; a change applies on the next deploy.

## Data

- **API Keys** — generate a key for your own tools; the key is shown once.
- **Connectors** — the AI assistants connected through OAuth, and a button to disconnect them all.
