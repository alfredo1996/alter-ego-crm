These pages belong to the **Consulting** plugin. They hold who you work for, what is signed and what is to do next. Every money figure is net of VAT, and the app keeps cash flow only: it never computes taxes.

## Pipeline

**Pipeline** lists the open opportunities and what they would be worth if they closed. A deal sits in one stage: lead, proposal or negotiation, then won or lost. Moving a deal to a stage seeds its probability (lead 20, proposal 50, negotiation 75), and you can set another. The weighted pipeline is the monthly value times the probability.

- A deal marked **lost** needs a reason.
- Marking a deal **won** does not create the contract. Use **Mark as won and create the contract** (or **Create Contract** from the Won column): it fills the rate, days per month, budget and dates from the deal.
- A deal that only adds to a contract already signed (more days, a new rate, a renewal) is not converted: record it as an amendment, or its days count twice.
- **Next follow-up** is the date that shows on the Deadlines calendar. A follow-up is never done: it happens and moves, or it goes overdue.

Tools: `get_pipeline`, `create_opportunity`, `move_opportunity`, `convert_to_engagement`, `schedule_follow_up`.

## Clients

**Clients** lists who you work for and what is signed and owed. A client name is unique, in any case. Open a client for its contracts, open opportunities, notes and the calls logged with it. Logging an opportunity for a name that does not exist creates the client.

Tools: `list_clients`, `get_client`, `create_client`, `add_note`, `log_meeting`, `update_meeting`, `list_meetings`.

## Contracts and amendments

A contract belongs to a client and is signed in **days** or in **hours** (a day rate or an hourly rate). It has:

- a rate, the days (or hours) per month and an optional budget of contracted days: leave the budget empty for no cap;
- a start month and an optional end: no end means it runs on;
- **VAT applicable**, off for reverse charge, abroad or exempt;
- an optional name, which tells it apart from the client's other contracts.

Editing a contract corrects it. A change the client signed (a new rate, more days, a renewal) is an **amendment** (**Amend Contract**): it applies from a month on, earlier months keep their price in the Plan and on invoices, and it is shown in the contract's history with a note such as the addendum or PO number. Rules you meet:

- one amendment per month: a second one for the same month replaces it;
- an amendment can only extend the end, never shorten it; to shorten, edit the contract;
- hours-based contracts have no day budget to add to;
- a change that would reprice a month already invoiced is refused with the difference per month; **Save and Reprice** confirms it. A rise is a top-up invoice, a cut a credit note;
- an amendment is removed with **Remove Amendment**, latest first.

Closing a contract keeps what it already earned: months up to its end still count in revenue and in the Plan.

Hours worked go in a timecard, by week. The 8-hour day holds: at most 8 hours a day for one client across its contracts, hours never pass a contract's days, and a contract closed with no end takes none. New hours are accepted from the contract's start month, up to 3 months ahead.

Tools: `convert_to_engagement`, `create_contract`, `update_engagement`, `amend_contract`, `log_time`, `list_time`, `get_hours_left`.

## Contracts timeline

**Contracts** (`/contracts`) draws every contract of every client on one timeline. Click a bar for the contract's days and money by quarter. It shows each contract's health (green, yellow or red, from the latest call that states one), the days logged and left, and flags a contract that is **Nearly full**. **Show Pipeline** adds the open deals, not signed yet. **Earlier** and **Later** move the window. From a bar you can open the contract's client, its opportunity or its solution.

Tools: `get_client` and `list_meetings` read the same health and calls.

## Collaborators

**Collaborators** lists the freelancers and companies the company pays on invoice, and what they cost. An employee is a company with a fixed fee taken from the accountant's figure: there is no payroll. Names are unique, in any case.

- An optional **fixed fee** is a monthly amount net of VAT, from a first month to an optional last month, with dated changes (one per month, after the start, not past the end).
- **Inactive** keeps the person listed but takes their fee and days out of the plan.
- An **assignment** puts a collaborator on a contract (one per collaborator and contract), with a cost per day (or per hour), days per month, a first month and an optional last month, never past the contract's end. Their days are part of the contract's days, never on top: the client is billed for all of them at the contract's rate, and the cost is their days times their rate.
- Removing an assignment is refused while their days are logged on the contract: set its end instead, so that what they worked still counts.

Capacity counts the owner's own days only: a collaborator's days on a contract are not the owner's.

Tools: `add_collaborator`, `update_collaborator`, `assign_collaborator`, `unassign_collaborator`.

## Tasks

**Tasks** is a board with the columns To Do, In Progress and Done. A task always belongs to a client and, optionally, to one of its contracts; it can carry a due date, notes and up to 10 labels. Open tasks come first, soonest due first, with an overdue flag. Completing a task keeps it in the history; a task added by mistake is deleted.

Tools: `list_tasks`, `add_task`, `update_task`, `complete_task`, `delete_task`.

## Deadlines

**Deadlines** is a calendar of the dates the company has to remember. Some you type in (the accountant's fee, an F24 the accountant gave you, a contract renewal, a bank formality), and the app shows the rest from your data every time: invoice due dates (**To collect**), pipeline follow-ups and budget exhaustion. Only the typed ones can be ticked off with **Mark Done** and reopened. The app computes no taxes: an F24 is there only if you stored it.

Tools: `list_deadlines`, `add_deadline`, `complete_deadline`.
